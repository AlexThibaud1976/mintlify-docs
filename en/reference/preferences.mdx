All user preferences are persisted via **ADO Extension Data** (user-scoped key), meaning they follow you across browsers and machines automatically.

Data is compressed with **LZString** before storage to fit within the 5 MB quota. In **My preferences**, nothing is written as you change it: each page keeps a draft, saved with **Save**.

**Preferences that sync:**
- Form templates (save/load)
- Field configuration (visibility, labels, custom fields)
- My preferences (colour, header and footer label, thresholds, sections and their order, logo)
- Language (FR/EN) — toggled in the top-right of TestPulse Studio
- Traceability view preference (table vs cards)
- Pass rate weighting (by config vs by test case)
- Max runs limit

**No global reset:** *My preferences › Sections and export* offers **Restore the delivered order**, which resets the section order only — not the ticked sections, the branding or the thresholds. **Cancel**, in the banner of a page, discards a draft that is not saved yet.
