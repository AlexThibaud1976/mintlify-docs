---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the extension">
    On the [Visual Studio Marketplace](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=AlexThibaud.TestPulse-Studio), click **Get it free**, choose your organization, then **Install** and accept the permission scopes. Installing an extension requires an organization administrator. See [Installation](/en/getting-started/installation) for the prerequisites.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open TestPulse Studio">
    In any project, open the **TestPulse Studio** group in the left navigation bar. It is a group of its own, visible even when the Test Plans service is turned off, with four areas: **Pilotage**, **Couverture**, **Conception** and **Outils**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate a report">
    In **Pilotage › Report**, select a test plan — its suites load and can be unticked —, fill in the report information, then click **Generate report**. Export it as PDF, Word, Excel, HTML or PowerPoint. See [Generating a report](/en/user-guide/generating).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go further">
    Follow the campaign in the [Cockpit](/en/user-guide/cockpit-tab), check [requirement coverage](/en/user-guide/coverage-builder), or set your own values in [My preferences](/en/settings/settings-navigation).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Optional: the AI assistant

The AI functions are **off when shipped**. A project administrator connects a model provider in **Project settings › Extensions › TestPulse Studio › AI assistant**, chooses which functions to turn on and which data may be sent. Until then, nothing is sent to any model. See [Security and AI](/en/security-and-ai).

## Coming from TestPulse?

**Import from TestPulse** (Outils › Settings) reads your TestPulse data and writes it to TestPulse Studio only — TestPulse is never modified. See [Import from TestPulse](/en/user-guide/testpulse-import).
