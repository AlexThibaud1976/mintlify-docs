The fields you fill in before generating a report, their label, and what appears on the cover page — **My preferences › Form fields**.

## Standard fields {#form-fields-settings}

One table, one row per field: **Shown** · **Field** · **Label shown** · **Required** · **Cover page** · **Expected format**.
- **Shown** — a hidden field is neither asked for nor printed
- **Label shown** — the label of the field in the form
- **Required** — a required field left empty blocks generation
- **Cover page** — whether the field is printed on the cover page
- **Expected format** — **Edit** opens it (see below)

The contacts (IT, business, tester, approver) are Azure DevOps identities: no format to check. The **Associated build** is chosen from a list: neither required nor format — hidden, the build selector disappears from the form and no build lookup is performed.

## Expected format {#validation-dialog}

**Edit**, in the *Expected format* column, opens what Studio checks while you fill in the field, before generating:
- **Advised length** — beyond it, an orange warning; never blocking, never truncated. Empty: no limit
- **Regular expression** — for example `^[A-Z]{2}-\d{4,6}$`. Empty: no format
- **Message when the entry does not match**
- **Prevent generation when the entry does not match** — otherwise an orange warning, and generation stays possible
- **Test a value** — type a value and see at once whether it matches

Validation runs in real time in the form (red or orange border) and again at generation time.

## Environment values {#env-values}

The values offered in the **Environment** field. Add or remove them; a value already offered is refused, and a value holds 20 characters at most.

## Additional fields {#custom-fields}

Up to **3 fields** specific to your reports, printed after the standard fields, in the form and in every export:
- **Label**
- **Type** — free text, or a list of choices (separated by commas)
- **Required field** — or not

Their values are included in the PDF cover page, the HTML header and the Word cover section.
