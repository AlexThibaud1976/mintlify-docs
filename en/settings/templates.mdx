Templates and views let you save and restore form configurations for recurring campaigns. They are saved and loaded from the report form, not from My preferences.

## Templates vs Views {#templates-views}

**Templates** — save metadata only (title, environment, tester, approver, etc.). Fast to load; plan selection is left to the user.

**Views** — save plan ID + selected suites + metadata. Loading a view re-selects the plan, waits for suites, then checks the saved intersection. Deleted suites are ignored silently.

Both types: **Save** (enter a name) → **Load** (one click) → **Delete**.
