How much history your reports read, and how they show suites — **My preferences › Execution**.

- **Runs analysed at most** — older ones beyond that are ignored. When the project or the profile of the plan sets the value, it is read with its lock; otherwise you choose between 100, 200, 500 or 1,000.
- **Empty suites** — **Show empty suites** displays the suites without any test point in the plan.

The pass-rate weighting is no longer here: it is in **Thresholds and calculations**.

## Runs analysed per test case {#max-runs}

Beyond 200, generation slows down on large plans, and the page warns you.

| Value | Use case | Warning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 100 | Small plans, fast generation |  |
| 200 | Standard (recommended) |  |
| 500 | Large plans with deep history | Slower generation |
| 1,000 | Exhaustive audit | ⚠ May be slow on large plans |
