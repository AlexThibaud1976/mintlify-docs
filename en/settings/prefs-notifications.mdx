Where to post a report once generated, and the generation reminders — **My preferences › Notifications and sending**.

- **Publishing channels** — **Microsoft Teams** or **Slack**, **3 at most** in all. The channels the project sets for the plan come first, read-only with their lock; yours follow, and you add or remove them. A webhook address lets anyone post in the channel: it is **masked** on screen, never shown in full, and must start with `https`. **Send a test** is offered on every channel, the ones set by the project included, and writes nothing. When the browser blocks the send (it can happen on Azure DevOps Server), Studio offers the message to copy and paste.
- **Generation reminders** — each line gives the plan, the frequency in words (every week, every two weeks, every month) and the **last report**. The reminders the project sets come first, read-only; you schedule or remove your own (**+ Schedule a reminder**: plan, frequency, day, saved view). Studio reminds you once the due date has passed, when it opens — no server sends the reminder: nothing reminds you while Studio is closed.

After a report is generated, its **Notify** button offers the project’s channels for the plan first, then yours.
