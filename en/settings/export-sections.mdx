What an exported report contains, in which order, and the formats offered in the export bar — **My preferences › Sections and export**.

- **Report sections** — **one list** gives both the sections included and their order: tick a section, drag its row by the handle or use the arrows (the keyboard works too). When the project sets which sections are included, the checkboxes are read with their lock — **the order remains yours**: the project can set which sections are included, never their order. **Restore the delivered order** resets the order only — not the ticked sections, the branding or the thresholds.
- **Optional sections** — charts (HTML), charts in the PDF, screenshots, step details, and the suite × configuration grid. Some may slow down generation, and the page says which.
- **Formats offered** — the buttons of the export bar: PDF, HTML, Word, Excel, PowerPoint. An unticked format is no longer offered; its settings stay in place.

## Section order {#section-order}

The 12 sections of the list keep the order you give them, saved with your preferences. The **Signature** row is set in **Branding and signature**. Without PDF among your formats, the order and the signature sheet stay saved but are no longer used — a note on the page says so.
