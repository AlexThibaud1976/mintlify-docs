**Where.** *Outils › Settings* opens **My preferences**: your own values, **except where the project or the profile of the selected plan sets its own**. The header reads **your project › Test Plans › TestPulse Studio › Preferences**, with a **Project member** (or **Project administrator**) pill and the line **Profile applied: X (plan “Y”)** — or **Project default** when the plan is bound to no profile.

**A side rail of seven pages, in three groups.** You land on **Thresholds and calculations**.
- **Report** — **Thresholds and calculations**, **Branding and signature**, **Sections and export**, **Form fields**.
- **Campaign** — **Execution**, **Notifications and sending**.
- **Project** — **Project rules**, read-only.

At the bottom of the rail, **Project administrators — Project settings ›** opens the administration for a project administrator; anyone else reads **Reserved for project administrators**.

**A value set by the project is read, never changed.** It carries a lock — **Set by the project** or **Set by the profile “X”** — and its provenance; a banner at the top of the page counts them, and **See the project rules** leads to the page that lists them. Your other values apply: a value set by the project never takes the others away from you.

**One way to save, as in the administration.** Each page keeps a **draft**. A sticky banner at the top says « N unsaved changes », with **Cancel** and **Save**; when the draft is invalid, **Save** is disabled and the reason is shown. Nothing is saved as you change it — switches and checkboxes included. Leaving a page with a draft, through the rail or by closing the window, asks « Leave without saving? ». A save that fails keeps your draft. Your preferences are saved in your Azure DevOps profile and follow you from one browser to another.

**No longer here.** The **Environment stages** group: the promotion decision ladder is set in *Project settings › Extensions › TestPulse Studio › Verdict and campaigns*, reserved for project administrators. The read-only **Test grid** and **AI assistant** groups: both are now in **Project rules**. **Signature**: now in **Branding and signature**. **Execution & advanced**: now **Execution**, and the pass-rate weighting moved to **Thresholds and calculations**. The **Project settings** banner and the footer that listed the scopes are gone too.
