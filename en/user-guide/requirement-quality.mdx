**Where.** **Pilotage › Requirement quality**. Choose an **Iteration**, an **Area path** (with or without its sub-paths) and a **Type**; the filters stay in the page address, so a link reopens the same view. **State considered**: *At start* (each story as it was on the iteration start date) or *Today*. **View**: *Iteration*, *Area paths* or *Efficiency*; **Compare with** another iteration writes the difference on each tile.

**What it shows.** Five tiles: **Definition of Ready met**, **Committed without DoR at start**, **Median score**, **Open critical questions**, **DoR at first assessment**. Then **Why the DoR is not met** (a story can trigger several rules), the INVEST criteria against the project target, and the **stories to handle** (DoR not met, outdated, unreadable or never assessed) — **Open** opens the work item, **Assess** opens step 0 of **Conception**. **Area paths** shows one row per child path, in the order of the paths, never as a ranking; a row with less than 60% of its stories assessed says so (“low assessed share”). **Compute the trend** reads the last six iterations, only when you ask. **Efficiency**: assessments before the DoR is met, days from creation to the DoR, improvements written to the story, designs made on an unmet DoR.

**Where the figures come from.** They are computed by TestPulse Studio from the **saved assessments**, each with the grid it was made under — never recomputed, never sent again to the AI assistant. Opening the page, reading it and exporting it make **no AI call**. The verdict counted is the last assessment of each story at the date considered. *At start* counts only the stories that were in the iteration on its start date: a story added since is left out of that view (the view says how many) and counted in *Today*; a story moved out since still counts at start and is listed “moved out since”. When the stories present at the start cannot be read, only *Today* is offered, and the page says so. The page is read when you open it or change a filter: it is **not real time**. Indicators appear once reading is complete, never on a partial sample; **Cancel reading** stops it and shows nothing until you choose **Read again**.

**Honest states.** Beyond 200 stories matching the filters, nothing is read (“312 stories match the filters (limit: 200)”): choose a narrower iteration or area path. Under five measured stories, raw counts replace percentages. An iteration without a start date offers only *Today*, and **Committed without DoR at start** is not computed. Unreadable histories are excluded and counted apart, with **Retry**. An information line under each view, repeated in each export, gives the number of stories measured, the rule counted, the grid used and “no AI call”.

**Exports.** **Export to CSV** (one row per story), **Export the area table (CSV)** and **PDF report** export what is shown, without reading again: comma-separated, UTF-8, the information line first. A CSV holds no model text beyond the labels of the rules. The PDF carries the project’s branding on its cover, the filters, the reading date, the state considered and each section shown, and states that it contains no new model opinion.

**What it does not do.** It ranks no one: no person field is read, and it groups by area path only. It writes nothing to Azure DevOps. It does not assess stories: assessments are made in the **Exigence** tab or at step 0 of **Conception**.
