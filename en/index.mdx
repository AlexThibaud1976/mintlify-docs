TestPulse Studio is an **Azure DevOps extension** for test teams. It lives in its own **TestPulse Studio** group of the project navigation and works on the data you already have in Azure Test Plans.

- **Report** on a campaign: a ready-to-share report (PDF, Word, Excel, HTML, PowerPoint) with a deterministic quality score and a GO / NO-GO verdict.
- **Follow** requirement coverage and drift, and run the morning stand-up of a campaign in the Cockpit.
- **Measure** requirement quality iteration by iteration — computed from the saved assessments, with no AI call.
- **Design** manual and automated tests from a requirement, with an optional AI assistant that proposes and never writes on its own.

<Note>
The figures, scores and verdicts are computed by TestPulse Studio; the AI explains them, it never produces them.
</Note>
